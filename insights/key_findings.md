# Key Business Findings — Retail Analytics Project

## Dataset
- 1,067,371 transactions | 5,942 unique customers | 43 countries
- Period: December 2009 – December 2011
- Total Revenue: £19,287,251 | Avg Order Value: £18.07

## Finding 1 — Champion Customers Drive Disproportionate Revenue
- 1,280 Champion customers (21.5% of base) generated £12,190,618 — 73.2% of total revenue
- Average Champion spends £9,524 vs £221 for Lost customers (43× difference)
- Recommendation: Prioritize retention programs for Champion segment

## Finding 2 — Q4 Seasonal Revenue Spike is Highly Predictable
- November 2010: +36.1% MoM revenue growth
- November 2011: +36.5% MoM revenue growth
- December drives consistently above-average order volumes
- Recommendation: Pre-position inventory and marketing spend by October

## Finding 3 — UK Market Dominates, International Opportunity Exists
- UK generates ~83% of total revenue
- Netherlands and Germany are strongest international markets
- 43 countries served but top 3 account for ~90% of revenue
- Recommendation: Targeted campaigns for Netherlands and Germany growth

## Finding 4 — 2,919 At-Risk/Lost Customers Represent £1.3M Recoverable Revenue
- At Risk: 1,322 customers | £979,492 revenue
- Lost: 1,597 customers | £352,267 revenue
- Combined recoverable opportunity: ~£1.33M
- Recommendation: Win-back email campaigns with discount incentives

## Finding 5 — K-Means Clustering Validated RFM Segments
- K-Means (k=4, silhouette score: 0.59) confirmed 4 behaviorally distinct groups
- Elbow method validated k=4 as optimal cluster count
- Cluster separation clearly visible on Recency vs Monetary scatter plot

## Finding 6 — Thursday is Peak Revenue Day
- Highest transaction volume and revenue consistently on Thursday
- Weekend orders significantly lower — Sunday lowest performing day
- Recommendation: Schedule promotions and email campaigns mid-week
